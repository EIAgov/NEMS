Annual Model Updates
====================

AEO2025 is the first time that CCATS has been included in NEMS. 
This section will be updated for future Annual Energy Outlooks.

CCATS replaced the Capture, Transport, Utilization, Storage (CTUS) module beginning with the 2025 Annual Energy Outlook (AEO2025).

Please look at these resources for additional information:

* `AEO2025 CCATS Fact Sheet <ttps://www.eia.gov/outlooks/aeo/resources/pdf/AEO2025_FactSheet_CCATS.pdf>`_
* Introduction to the Carbon Capture, Allocation, Transportation, and Sequestration Module

   * `Meeting summary <https://www.eia.gov/aeo/workinggroup/pdf/AEO2025_CCATS_Memo.pdf>`_
